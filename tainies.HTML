GAME OF THE WEEK

Minecraft

Minecraft remains one of the most flexible games ever created because it gives players an almost unlimited number of ways to play. You can build enormous structures, explore dangerous dimensions, create automated farms, fight bosses, play with friends or simply start a new survival world and see where it takes you. Its strongest feature is freedom: the game does not force you into one particular style. Years after release, the community continues to create servers, maps, challenges and completely new ways to experience the game.
